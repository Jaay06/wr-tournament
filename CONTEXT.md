# Domain glossary

This glossary defines the product language used in the codebase and documentation.

## Tournament

The single private competition currently managed by the app. The MVP does not support multiple tournaments.

## Tournament invite

A private link or code that lets an authenticated friend join the tournament. The organizer can close or replace it.

## Participant

An authenticated friend who has joined the tournament through its invite.

## Player registration

A participant's tournament details: Riot ID, current rank, self-assessed tier, organizer-approved tier, and primary and secondary role preferences.

## Organizer

The person who operates the tournament, reviews tiers, manages settings and teams, and posts announcements. There is one organizer role and no administrator hierarchy.

## Team

A tournament roster led by a captain. A team contains exactly five starters and may contain up to two substitutes when submitted.

## Captain

A team member who manages the roster, sends invitations, handles join requests, and submits the team.

## Starter

One of the five active roster positions on a submitted team.

## Substitute

An optional reserve member. A submitted team may have no more than two substitutes.

## Tier

One of four balancing categories, T1 through T4, assigned from rank and confirmed by the organizer.

## Approved tier

The organizer-confirmed tier used when validating a team for submission.

## Valid roster

A roster with exactly five starters, no more than two substitutes, approved tiers for every member, no more than one T1 player, and no more than two T2 players.

## Submitted team

A valid roster that its captain has entered into the tournament before the registration deadline. Submission freezes participant editing unless the organizer unlocks the team.

## Role preference

A player's primary or secondary Wild Rift role: Baron, Jungle, Mid, Dragon, or Support. Role coverage is advisory and does not determine whether a roster is valid.

## Announcement

A message posted by the organizer and visible to tournament participants inside the app.
